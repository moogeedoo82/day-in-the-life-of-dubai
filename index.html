Methodology and evidence

The 1,000 simulated residents are calibrated to Dubai's official population age structure and scaled to the latest population figure used in this project. Employment and education roles are calibrated using published labour-force and education indicators. Exact minute-by-minute behavior is generated from documented assumptions because Dubai does not publish an ATUS-style public time-diary microdataset.
